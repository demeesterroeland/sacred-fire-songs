Design Tokens & Components v2

Sober greyscale foundation for Sacred Fire Songs. Derives directly from the ChordPro reader experience (Report §5).

1. Colour Tokens & Contrast

Greys provide structure and hierarchy. --accent is reserved exclusively for primary actions, the "now playing" indicator, and chord notation symbols. No rainbow categories.

--bg Base canvas Canvas
--surface Cards & bars Layer 1
--surface-2 Toggles & pills Layer 2
--line Subtle borders Hairlines
--text Primary text ≥ 16:1 AAA Pass
--text-muted Secondary text ≥ 6.8:1 AA Pass
--text-subtle Meta & captions ≥ 5.2:1 AA Pass
--accent Fire orange ≥ 5.2:1 Key hue
--danger Destructive ≥ 4.6:1 AA Pass
