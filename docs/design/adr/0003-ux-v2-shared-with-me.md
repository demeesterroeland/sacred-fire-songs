## Context

Recipients of a shared take had no inbox (R5) and shared takes were mixed with their own (R4).

## Decision

- **Both**: a separate "Shared with me" list (G4.2, #399) **and** the existing notification bell (Story 4.6.6, #373).

## Consequences

- Two entry points to the same data; the list is the durable view, the bell the timely nudge.
