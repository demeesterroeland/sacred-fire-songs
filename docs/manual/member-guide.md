Everything in the [Guest Guide](guest-guide.md) applies. This page covers what an account adds. Practising and recordings have their own page: [practice-and-recordings.md](practice-and-recordings.md) (the player bar shows only after you press play on a reference track or a take).

## Your account menu

Tap your initial (top right). You see your name and role (e.g. *Member*), and:

- **Account Settings**, **Your Favorites**, **My Drafts**, **My Playlists**, **Shared with me** (with "3 new" when there are new takes), **Recently Viewed**
- **Language** and the light / dark / automatic theme switch
- **Sign Out**

## Home when signed in

Above the categories, **Your library** shows four counters: **Favorites**, **Drafts**, **Your Songs**, **New songs**. Tap one to open that list.

## Favourites

Tap **Like** in the bar at the bottom of the song page. The heart fills and the label reads **Liked**. Remove it the same way ("Remove from Liked Songs"). All favourites are in **Library → Your Favorites**.

## Library

Tabs at the top: **Playlists**, **History**, **Artists**. Under the tabs, the row **Shared with me** opens the takes others shared with you.

### Shared with me

When someone shares a take with you, the bell at the top shows a number. **Shared with me** (account menu, or Library) lists every take shared with you: the song, *Shared by* the person's name and the take. A take you have not opened yet says **New**. Tap a row to open the song with that take highlighted. Opening a song's **Practice** panel marks that song's new takes as read, and the number on the bell goes down. Guests are asked to sign in.

**Smart playlists** are filled automatically:

| Smart playlist | Contains |
| :--- | :--- |
| Your Favorites | Songs you liked |
| Your Songs | Songs you added |
| My Drafts | Your songs that are not public yet |
| Your takes | Songs where you have practice takes |

Below that: **My private playlists** and **Recently viewed** ("View all" opens the full History).

## Playlists (setlists)

**Create:** bottom bar **Library → +  → New Playlist**, or **Library → + New Playlist**. Type a name → **Create Playlist**. New playlists are private; you can change that later.

**Add songs:**
- From a song: **Playlist** in the bar at the bottom → pick a playlist.

- From a song: ⋮ → **Add to Playlist**. A sheet slides up: **New playlist** is the first row, then your playlists. A check shows where the song already is. Tap a playlist to add the song; tap a playlist with a check to take it out again. **Done** closes the sheet.
- From the playlist: **+ Add Songs** and search.

**In a playlist** you can: reorder songs by dragging, add a description, switch **public / private** (lock icon), **print / export PDF** (printer icon) for the ceremony, share the link of a public playlist, and duplicate it via ⋯ **(verify menu labels)**.

The PDF has one song per section with chords, and a footer with the site address and a QR code to each song. Exporting the same unchanged playlist again reuses the earlier PDF, so it is fast.

## Adding a song

Bottom bar **Library → + → Add Song**.

1. **Song Title** (required) and **Original Author / Artist** (optional; you can add several).
2. **Lyrics & chords:** put chords in square brackets right before the syllable: `[Dm]Abre tus alas [A7]pájaro volar`. Chords written on a line above the lyrics are converted automatically. The **?** shows help.
3. Or tap **Or upload a file (.cho, .txt)** to import a ChordPro file.
4. Lines like `{title: …}`, `{artist: …}`, `{key: E}`, `{capo: 3}` and `{c: comment}` set the song details.
5. Add YouTube / Spotify / SoundCloud links so others can listen.
6. Save. Your draft is also kept in the browser while you type.

## Editing a song

Open your song → ⋮ → **Edit** **(verify label)**. You can only edit songs you added. Others see *This Medicine is Not Yours to Edit*. Gatekeepers and admins can edit details of any song.

## Settings

**Account Settings** has tabs:

- **Profile:** display name. E-mail changes go via support.
- **Security:** change password, log out from all devices.
- **Preferences:** appearance, language, keep screen awake, auto-hide navigation on song pages and e-mail notifications (text size is set on the song page with A− / A+, auto-scroll with ▶ and ⋯ next to them).

## Public profile

Members and musicians have a profile page (`/users/…`). Singer attributions on recordings link to it. Guests are asked to sign in to see profiles.
