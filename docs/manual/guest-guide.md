## What Sacred Fire Songs is

A songbook of medicine and ceremony songs with lyrics and chords, for singing together in a circle. Anyone can read and listen. With a free account you can also save favourites, make playlists, add songs and record your own practice takes.

## Finding your way

The screen has three fixed parts:

- **Top bar:** ☰ menu (left), the search field ("Search 200+ songs") with a filter button, and **Sign In** (right).
- **Content** in the middle.
- **Bottom bar:** **Home**, **Search**, **Library**. **Search** opens the song list and puts the cursor in the search field.

The ☰ menu repeats the main places (Home, Songs, Playlists, Favorites, Drafts, Recently Viewed, Add Song, New Playlist) and has the **language switch** at the bottom.

## Finding a song

1. **Browse by category** on Home: groups such as *The Elements* (Air, Earth, Fire, Water), *Nature*, *Lineage & Tradition*, *Medicine & Healing*. Each tag is a plain link; the grey number after it is how many songs it holds. "+3 more" shows the rest.
2. **Search:** tap the search field and type a title, a line of the lyrics, an author or a category. Accents do not matter ("aguita" finds "Agüita").
3. **Filters:** the slider button in the search field opens *Search options*: sort order, content type (songs with chords / with melody) and category & tag filters. Tap **Show results**.
4. **Search** in the bottom bar lists all songs A–Z and is ready for typing. Each row shows the title, a grey line *author · category · language* and the key on the right; tap the row to open the song.
5. **Library → Artists** lists every author with their number of songs.

## Reading a song

- The title is at the top. Under it, one grey line shows the author and the tags (category, tradition, language); tap it to see them all as links.
- A second line shows **Key**, **Capo** and **Tuning**, e.g. `Key E · Capo 3 · Standard`.
- Under those lines: **Chords** switches the chords on or off, **A−** / **A+** make the text smaller or larger. The app remembers your choice on this device.
- Chords are printed above the word where they change.
- ⋮ (top right) opens the song menu: like, share, add to playlist, Practice. Actions that need an account ask you to sign in.

## Listening to the song

Open ⋮ → **Practice** to open the Practice panel.

- **Listen** tab: choose **YouTube**, **SoundCloud** or **Spotify** (when the song has that link) and play it inside the app.
- A small **player bar** appears above the bottom bar once you press play (a song page never shows it before), so you can keep listening while reading. Use ⟲ / ⟳ to jump back or forward, ✕ to stop. Leaving the song page stops the music.
- Tip: wear headphones if you sing along while recording later.

The **Record** tab shows what members can do and a **Sign In to Record** button.

## Things that need an account

Favourites, playlists, recently viewed, adding or editing songs, recording, account settings, singer profiles. Each of these screens shows the same grey preview with a lock, one sentence saying why it is locked and a **Sign in** button; after signing in you land on the screen you came from. Guests will be able to listen to public takes that the singer agreed to publish (Practice → Listen); recording, sharing and downloading stay members-only. The list of takes for guests follows in a later update. You can open the **Add Song** form and start typing as a guest: a grey box at the top says **Draft now — sign in to save**: saving a song needs an account. Your draft stays on this device, so after you sign in (from the box or when you press save) you are back on the form with your text still there.

## Creating an account

1. Tap **Sign In** → **Join the Circle** (or go to *Create Account*).
2. Enter e-mail and a password (twice) → **Create Account**.
3. Check your e-mail: click the link **or** type the **6-digit code** → **Verify & Continue**.
4. On *Welcome at the Fire* enter the name others will see → **Finish Setup & Explore**.

## Signing in

- **Send Magic Link:** enter your e-mail, open the link in the mail. No password needed.
- **Sign in with password:** tap the button, enter e-mail and password.
- Forgot your password? **Reset Password** sends a reset link; then choose a new password on *New Password*.

## Install as an app

The site can be installed on your phone's home screen ("Add to Home Screen" / "Install app") and then opens full screen with the Sacred Fire Songs icon **(verify the exact prompt per browser)**.

## No internet?

If you lose connection you see **You're offline**. The page reloads by itself when you are back online, or tap **Try again**.

## A link that does not work

If a web address does not exist, you see **Page not found** with a **Go home** button. You are not sent to the sign-in page. Pages that need an account (for example Account settings) still ask you to sign in.

## Language

Choose your language in the ☰ menu (English, Español, Português, Français, Deutsch, Nederlands, Italiano, עברית). Song titles and lyrics are not translated.
